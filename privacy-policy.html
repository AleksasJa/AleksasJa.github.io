Privacy Policy

App: Lesta YouTube Automation  ·  Owner: the owner of the YouTube channel ja888d | WOT Replays  ·  Last updated: 10 October 2026

What the app does

This is a private tool used only by the channel owner on their own computer. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's own channel, edit their titles, descriptions and playlists, and post or reply to comments on that channel.

Data

  • The app accesses only the owner's own YouTube account, with the owner's permission.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are not shared with anyone.
  • The app does not collect, sell or share personal data of other people. Public comments are read only to reply to them on the channel.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: via the channel's Discord or the support e-mail shown on the Google consent screen.
