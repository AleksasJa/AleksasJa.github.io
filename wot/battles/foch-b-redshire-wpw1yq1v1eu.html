Foch B on Redshire — 9,916 damage, 6 kills, 3,762 assisted, Ace Tanker. 24 hits from 27 shots and every single one penetrated — then one clip takes out two of the last three enemies to close a 15–9 win.

Player: Omega275 · original replay

Battle breakdown

How the battle played out Omega275 takes the Foch B to Redshire and turns it into a clinic in efficiency: 27 shots, 24 hits and every single one of those hits penetrated. That is what separates a 9.9K game from a 5K game in a clip-loading tank destroyer — the Foch B fires a burst of shells and then sits through a long reload, so a bounced or missed shell is damage you never get back.

The first clear swing comes mid-battle, when a single shell sends a Vz. 55 up in flames. A few minutes later the Foch empties one clip into an Obj. 705A for more than 2,200 damage and deletes it — the moment our first Short is built around.

The finish With three enemies left, the Foch fires one clip and two of them go down. Then it reloads and hunts the last one to close out a 15–9 win. Along the way it adds 3,762 assisted damage on top of the 9,916 it dealt itself, and earns Ace Tanker.

What to take from it Clip tank destroyers win by choosing when to unload: a full burst into a target that cannot hide or answer in time, then the long reload spent somewhere safe. A 100% penetration rate over 24 hits shows how carefully each shot was picked.
