Durendal on Mines — 10,039 damage, 3 kills, Ace Tanker. 42 shots, 32 hits, 28 penetrations: the new tier X French medium from the Assembly Shop fires two shells per shot, and here you see exactly why that gun is so scary. Opening burst, a mirror duel against an enemy Durendal, and a win at the end.

Player: BilliamFrampton · original replay

Battle breakdown

How the battle played out The Durendal is the tier X French medium from the Assembly Shop, and its gimmick is the gun: every shot fires two shells. BilliamFrampton shows why that matters on Mines — 42 shots, 32 hits, 28 penetrations and 10,039 damage.

The opening is brutal. Two double-taps in the first minute deal 738 damage to an AVR FS and then 707 to a Projet Louis, which goes down — 1,445 damage in two trigger pulls.

The mirror duel Around four minutes in, an enemy Durendal shows up. Mirror matches come down to who lands both shells of the double-tap first: one burst for 938 damage decides it, and the enemy Durendal is the one that does not drive away.

The finish Late in the game a WT auf Pz. IV is caught and destroyed, and the team closes the battle out with a win. Only three kills, but more than 10K damage and Ace Tanker — a game won by trading efficiently, not by chasing kills.

What to take from it With a double-tap gun the second shell is where the value is. Peek, land both, and get back into cover before the reload — repeated for twelve minutes, that adds up to five digits of damage.
