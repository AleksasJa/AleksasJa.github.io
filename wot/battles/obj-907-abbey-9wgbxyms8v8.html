Battle breakdown

How the battle played out The Obj. 907 is a Soviet tier X medium tank, and on Abbey it ends up doing the work of a whole team. The battle swings against it: the score is down 8–11 before three kills in under a minute put the team back in front.

Last one alive vs 3 Then the rest of the team is gone. It is the 907 alone against three enemies, and it finishes all three for the win.

The result 7,207 damage, 7 kills and 2,200 damage blocked, with Ace Tanker and Top Gun.

What to take from it A 1 vs 3 is won by refusing to fight all three at once. Using Abbey's buildings and ridges to isolate one enemy at a time — and letting the armour absorb what does come in — turns a lost game into a win.
