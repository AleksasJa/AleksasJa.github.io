Battle breakdown

How the battle played out Nemesis on Prokhorovka — 9,179 damage in a battle the enemy team will not forget. Prokhorovka is a wide open summer map split by a railway embankment, with fields, bushes and long sightlines, so tanks that sit still in a good spot can deal huge damage without being seen.

Positioning over everything On a map this open, damage comes from where you stand more than from how fast you shoot. Good bushes, the railway line as cover and timing your shots for when enemies are already spotted by teammates — that is how a game like this reaches 9K.

What to watch for Watch the full battle for where the Nemesis chooses to stop and shoot, and when it decides to move. Every change of position comes before the enemy can answer — that is why they never understood what hit them.
